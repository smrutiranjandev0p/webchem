SIMULATION 11 · FERTILIZER PROCESS

Urea synthesis · simplified material balance

Explore the stoichiometric feed balance for 2NH₃ + CO₂ → CO(NH₂)₂ + H₂O. Enter feed rates in kmol/h and an assumed overall conversion. This is a teaching model—not a plant design or equilibrium simulator.

At 2:1 NH₃:CO₂ feed, CO₂ is the stoichiometric reference.
—Urea produced · kmol/h
—Urea produced · kg/h
—Water formed · kg/h

Stoichiometry: 2 mol NH₃ react with 1 mol CO₂ to form 1 mol urea and 1 mol water.

Real urea plants involve equilibrium, high pressure/temperature, carbamate recycle, stripping and energy integration. This calculator intentionally excludes those process effects.

These are educational simulations, not validated laboratory procedures. Do not use them as instructions for handling chemicals. Real laboratory work requires trained supervision, PPE and approved protocols.
